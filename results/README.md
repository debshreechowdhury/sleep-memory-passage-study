# Outputs

Pipeline outputs go in results/quality/ and results/detection/. The completed report's aggregates are at the top level of results/.

quality/power_scenarios.csv is an assumption-based calculation, not participant data. Generated with Python 3.10, NumPy 2.2.6 and SciPy 1.15.3 using scripts/quality/power.py --n 30. Two-sided alpha .05, target power .80; required complete pairs for dz .3/.5/.8 are 90/34/15. At 30 complete pairs, a dz of about .5292 is detectable with .80 power. Recruitment allowance for exclusions is not included. These calculations were prepared after the form launch and are not evidence of prospective preregistration.

quality/power_achieved.csv repeats those scenarios at the achieved 26 pairs for alpha .05 and .0167, using the same NumPy and SciPy versions under Python 3.13. Assumed effects only; no observed power.

quality/quality.json and quality/regression.json hold the aggregate quality results for the 64-submission workbook under the student-only, both-checks rule (26 retained), with the earlier rule as a sensitivity. quality/hypothesis_tests.json holds the three hypothesis tests at .05/3, with the H1 sensitivity analyses and assumed-rate power. quality/*.png are their figures. These outputs contain no row-level data and identify the source workbook by SHA-256. The report's main results come from these files, and detection/detection.json repeats overall detection under the same rule. The top-level results below describe the earlier 54-response snapshot, which the report keeps as a sensitivity analysis.

Aggregate participant results are committed in report_statistics.json (54 exported responses, 49 retained). report_power.json contains retrospective planning scenarios at alpha .05 and .025. The report figures summarize these results and the paired-difference distribution. These aggregate files contain no row-level data; the de-identified row-level files are in `data/`. Core pipeline validation uses temporary synthetic CSVs and checks mapping, de-identification, mean difference, detection counts, chi-square and changed-header rejection. Synthetic records are deleted with their temporary directory after each test.

Debshree’s exploratory group/confidence aggregates are in snapshot_group_statistics.json; group_power.json gives exact Fisher planning scenarios under assumed rates. presentation_chart_data.json supplies all native slide charts. Figures now live in results/figures, shared by the report and presentation. All aggregate participant outputs identify the same private-source checksum. No identities, free text or row-level responses are included.

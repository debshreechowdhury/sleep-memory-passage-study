# Pilot protocol — complete before participant recruitment

The study team should test both Google Forms after the instructor or ethics contact has reviewed the brief nondisclosure and the analysis plan has been finalized. Use 3–4 pilot volunteers who are not included in the final analysis.

## For each form version

1. Open Preview on a computer and phone. Confirm the first passage is the correct version and both passages use matching text style.
2. Try to proceed without affirming each eligibility and consent check. The form should require all three affirmative choices.
3. Complete the ratings and comprehension check on both passage pages. Confirm all four questions are required on each page and the 1–7 endpoint labels are visible on phone screens.
4. Confirm the reveal repeats the two passages in the order seen, the detection choice is forced, confidence has five choices, and the explanation is optional.
5. Confirm AI-tool use and English proficiency appear after the reveal.
6. Confirm the debrief identifies the correct passage order, includes the NIH source and both researcher emails, and explains withdrawal before submission.
7. Record the overall completion time externally and note any confusing wording, layout issue, or accidental use of the Back button. Google Forms does not record passage-page time.

## After the pilot

- Record the pilot date, form version, device type, completion time, and issues in the team’s private notes. Do not place pilot volunteers’ names or contact details in this repository.
- Fix any survey issues in **both** form versions, then update `forms/instrument.md` and `forms/google-forms.md` in a reviewed PR.
- Recheck the final forms, publish them only after approval, and retain the final responder links and assignment method in team records.
- Keep pilot responses separate from the main study. If test responses were submitted, remove or label them before exporting study data.

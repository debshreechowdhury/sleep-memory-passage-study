### Stimulus, design and sampling

The most important limitation is fixed order. Because the AI passage was always first, detection correctness is the same as choosing Passage 1, so the H1 group difference cannot be separated from a group difference in preferring the first passage. Fixed AI-first order also confounds version with presentation position and permits carryover, familiarity and contrast effects. Both passages repeat the same information, so the second exposure may benefit comprehension or familiarity irrespective of authorship. One science topic and one rewrite do not represent human or AI prose generally. The texts share a direct scientific quotation, and the model, version and full generation prompt were not preserved in the export. Plain formatting reduced external source cues but did not remove textual NIH references.

Recruitment was voluntary through a shared link, with a convenience sample, and the student groups are small and were not randomized. Ten of the 26 analyzed responses arrived within about 15 minutes, and H1 does not reach the threshold without them; some of those respondents may have been recruited together or discussed the study, which would violate independence. The sample cannot estimate population-wide detection performance. Eligibility and the name question changed during launch, and one record lacks verified passage assignment. Student-background and current-status answers did not align exactly, and analyses restricted to stated Student status have different denominators. Background must not be used as a proxy for citizenship, nationality or language proficiency.

The workbook does not establish that collection ended. The group comparison and its test were specified before collection, but the written plan labelled it exploratory, and the decision to report it first, the three-test threshold, and the power sensitivities are retrospective and cannot be described as preregistered. Instructor or ethics approval for initial nondisclosure is not established by the available records and is not claimed. These limits should remain visible in both the written report and presentation.

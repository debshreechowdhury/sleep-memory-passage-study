### H1: Student-background findings

In this sample, international students chose the AI passage far more often than domestic students. The result supports a difference between these groups in what they chose, and it is the most interesting finding of the study, but it does not establish that international students are better at detecting AI.

Three things limit that claim. First, the AI passage was always first, so a correct answer was the same as choosing Passage 1; the groups may differ in their tendency to pick the first passage and not in their ability to recognize AI. Second, the groups are small, the result weakens without the last ten responses, and those responses arrived close together, so they may not be independent. Third, the written plan labelled this comparison exploratory, so it is better treated as a strong lead for a randomized follow-up than as a settled result.

Student background was observed and not assigned, so the comparison cannot establish that background caused the difference. Background also does not measure English proficiency, which was not collected.
